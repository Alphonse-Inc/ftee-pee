# Assumptions

Choices made because the brief stopped at the hosting rules, and every price that is not a confirmed FTEE-PEE tariff. Research checked on 18 November 2026. Figures below are other companies’ published lists or reported ranges. They are stored as sample packages the admin can edit. The public site labels them Sample and the primary action is “Request a quote”.

## Company facts we did not invent

- Legal name on the site: FTEE-PEE NIG LTD. Head office: Plus Code J9M2+P2C, Aguda, Surulere, Lagos 101233, Nigeria. No street number was provided, so none is shown. Area is Aguda. The address line is Surulere.
- No phone, email, WhatsApp number, logo file, opening hours, founding year, CAC/RC number, or staff count was provided. Those fields are empty. Hours are hidden until an admin sets them. The header uses a self-drawn wordmark until a logo is uploaded.
- No latitude or longitude is published. The map link is a Google Maps search for the Plus Code and place name. It does not use a paid Maps API key.
- Only the head office is seeded. More branches are added in the admin.
- No certifications, awards, client names, or statistics are shown. There is no “500+ clients” line.
- One testimonial row is seeded as sample text, unpublished, and flagged sample. The public query shows a quote only when it is published and not sample. Sample quotes never appear, even if someone ticks Published and leaves Sample on.

## How the four services are described

These are operating assumptions, written so an admin can change the copy:

- Design and print: artwork and printing. Collection at the Aguda office. Delivery only when the quote includes it.
- Cleaning: at the customer’s home or office. Surulere is the home area. Work further away is confirmed per quote. Fumigation is not a standard service.
- Wheel balancing and alignment: in the bay at the branch, not at the roadside.
- Car wash and detailing: at the branch. A visit to the customer’s address is not promised.
- Payment terms are agreed per job. No deposit percentage is invented.

Every seeded package except “Artwork” (price on request, not a number) has `is_sample = 1` and this note: “Sample price from publicly listed Lagos rates. Not a confirmed FTEE-PEE tariff.”

The market spread is wide. A lower advertised card price and a premium card price can both be “real” for different paper. The samples sit in the middle of what was published, not at the cheapest teaser.

## Product choices

- One PHP app, one CSS file, one small JS file. Fraunces and Outfit are self-hosted WOFF2 files under the SIL Open Font License (`assets/fonts/OFL-Fraunces.txt`, `assets/fonts/OFL-Outfit.txt`). No font CDN.
- Visual system: warm paper, ink, brass, and a rust registration mark, so print, cleaning, and auto care share one identity without a generic purple template.
- Web root is the project root. `app/`, `config/`, `database/`, and `templates/` are denied over HTTP.
- Session cookie: HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS, path from `base_url`.
- CSRF on every state-changing form. Passwords use `password_hash` / `password_verify`. Login attempts are counted in MySQL (5 per IP or 8 per email in 15 minutes).
- Uploads: JPG, PNG, WebP, 3 MB, random filename, pixel cap. PHP is not accepted. `uploads/.htaccess` blocks script extensions and does not use `php_flag` (that directive 500s the folder on many hosts). GD resize to 1800 px on the long edge when GD exists.
- Page body is plain text with paragraphs, escaped on output. Admins cannot inject HTML.
- Blog posts are educational notes in the company’s voice, dated when the site is installed. They are not presented as press coverage.
- Privacy and terms follow the shape of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 notice (purpose, recipients, retention, complaint to the NDPC) and say they are not a legal opinion. The site does not claim NDPC registration.
- Enquiry mail status is `skipped`, `sent`, or `failed`. Skipped means delivery is off or no recipient is set.
- Opening hours are omitted from JSON-LD so a free-text hour line is not guessed into a structured schedule.
- `dev-router.php` exists only for PHP’s built-in server. Production uses `.htaccess`.
